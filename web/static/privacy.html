WX Viewer Privacy

WX Viewer does not use advertising trackers, marketing pixels, or analytics cookies.

What This Site Stores

The site serves weather imagery, model maps, radar products, and related JavaScript assets. Normal web-server logs may record basic request information such as IP address, requested path, and timestamp for operational and security purposes.

Cookies

WX Viewer is not designed around non-essential cookies. If your browser stores anything for the site, it should only be what is required for normal delivery or browser-side functionality.

Third-Party Requests

The site may request some technical assets from third-party CDNs, including JavaScript libraries. Those providers may receive your IP address and standard request metadata as part of delivering those files.

Contact

If you have a privacy question about WX Viewer, use the main site contact method associated with vibeprojects.us.
