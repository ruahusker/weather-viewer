About this view

Real reflectivity observations from full radar scans. Heights are in feet above each radar antenna. Radar echoes can include insects, birds, and ground clutter. Unsampled regions are left empty.

When both sites are shown, overlap uses the nearer radar. Each site's observation time is shown separately. New complete scans appear automatically.
